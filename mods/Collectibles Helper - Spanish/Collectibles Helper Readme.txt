     1. Manual Installation

*****************************************************************

1. Unzip the contents of Collectibles Helper.zip to your Skyrim Data folder. Below is a list
   of the default Skyrim installation locations:

	C:\Program Files\Steam\steamapps\common\skyrim\data

			or for 64-bit Windows:

	C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\skyrim\data

2. Confirm you have the following files in your Skyrim Data folder:

	Collectibles Helper.esp
	Collectibles Helper.bsa

3. Run the Skyrim Launcher and make sure Collectibles Helper.esp appears in Data Files and
   has a tick next to it.

4. Start the game.

     2. Description

*****************************************************************

Toggle quest markers for the Stones of Barenziah, Jiub's Opus, East Empire Pendants or Ancient Falmer
Books. Or automatically collect these items with one click of a button. Collectibles Helper makes no
edits to the collectible quests and is compatible with other mods that also affect No Stone Unturned, 
Impatience of a Saint, Pain in the Necklace, or Forgotten Vale Book Quest.

Show Quest Markers: Toggle the Show Map Markers option in the MCM to put a quest marker over all
collectibles to make it easier to hunt down the Stones of Barenziah, pages of Jiub's Opus, East Empire
Necklaces, or Ancient Falmer Tomes. When you enable this option it'll add a new Miscellaneous objective
to your journal pointing out the location of any undiscovered collectibles.

Remove Collectible Quests: Includes an option to simply disable these collectibles and their quest
objectives for those of you who don't want to even interact with them. You can re-enable them later if
ever you change your mind.

Just Find Them For Me: Click on a button in the MCM to move all undiscovered Stones of Barenziah,
pages of Jiub's Opus, or Ancient Falmer Tomes straight into your inventory. Perfect for those of you who
feel utterly defeated by these kinds of quests and just want the cycle of misery to come to an end.

An MCM is included that allows you to toggle all features added by this mod.

     3. Configuration

*****************************************************************

Collectibles Helper includes an MCM that allows you to configure the following options:

- Disable Stones of Barenziah:
  Controls the enable state of Stones of Barenziah and whether or not the associated quest objectives
  are visible in your journal. When this is ticked, the Stones of Barenziah will not be visible and
  associated quest objectives will be removed from your journal. When this is unticked, the Stones of
  Barenziah will be made visible again and associated quest objectives will be re-added to your journal.

- Show Stones of Barenziah Map Markers:
  Ticking this adds a miscellaneous objective to your journal called 'Collectibles Helper - Stones of
  Barenziah'. When this objective is enabled, it'll point out all the locations of any Stones of
  Barenziah that are not already in your inventory. Even stones that you've previously collected but
  somehow misplaced will be pointed out.

- Put Undiscovered Stones in Inventory
  Clicking on this automatically puts all undiscovered Stones of Barenziah into your inventory. Keep in
  mind that this action can only be done once and is not reversible. 

- Disable Jiub's Opus:
  Controls the enable state of the pages of Jiub's Opus and whether or not the associated quest
  objectives are visible in your journal. When this is ticked, the pages will not be visible and
  associated quest objectives will be removed from your journal. When this is unticked, the pages will
  be made visible again and associated quest objectives will be re-added to your journal.

- Show Jiub's Opus Map Markers:
  Ticking this adds a miscellaneous objective to your journal called 'Collectibles Helper - Jiub's Opus'.
  When this objective is enabled, it'll point out all the locations of any pages that are not already in
  your inventory. Even pages that you've previously collected but somehow misplaced will be pointed out.

- Put Undiscovered Pages in Inventory
  Clicking on this automatically puts all undiscovered pages of Jiub's Opus into your inventory. Keep in
  mind that this action can only be done once and is not reversible.

- Disable Ancient Falmer Books:
  Controls the enable state of Ancient Falmer Books and whether or not the associated quest objectives
  are visible in your journal. When this is ticked, the Ancient Falmer Books will not be visible and
  associated quest objectives will be removed from your journal. When this is unticked, the Ancient
  Falmer Books will be made visible again and associated quest objectives will be re-added to your
  journal.

- Show Book Map Markers:
  Ticking this adds a miscellaneous objective to your journal called 'Collectibles Helper - Ancient
  Falmer Books'. When this objective is enabled, it'll point out all the locations of any Ancient Falmer
  Books that are not already in your inventory.

- Put Undiscovered Ancient Falmer Books in Inventory
  Clicking on this automatically puts all undiscovered Ancient Falmer Books into your inventory. Keep in
  mind that this action can only be done once and is not reversible. 

- Disable East Empire Pendants:
  Controls the enable state of East Empire Pendants and whether or not the associated quest objectives
  are visible in your journal. When this is ticked, the East Empire Pendants will not be visible and
  associated quest objectives will be removed from your journal. When this is unticked, the East Empire
  Pendants will be made visible again and associated quest objectives will be re-added to your journal.

- Show Pendant Map Markers:
  Ticking this adds a miscellaneous objective to your journal called 'Collectibles Helper - East
  Empire Pendants'. When this objective is enabled, it'll point out all the locations of any East
  Empire Lockboxes that contain East Empire Pendants.

     5. F.A.Q

*****************************************************************

Q. What patch level is required?
A. 1.6 for Legendary Edition. No particular requirement for Special/Anniversary Edition.

Q. Do I need Dawnguard, Hearthfire or Dragonborn or additional plugins like SKSE?
A. If you're on Legendary Edition you'll need Dawnguard. You will also need SKSE and SkyUI to use the
   MCM that comes with the mod in order to toggle the options added by this mod.

Q. Is this mod stable? / compatible with ### / affect performance?
A. Collectibles Helper doesn't edit the collectible quests to avoid compatibility issues with mods that
  do. Everything is handled by a single script.

Q. Aren't there already mods out there that do this kind of thing?
A. There are but they tend to edit the collection quests directly which may lead to compatibility
   problemswith other mods that also affect those quests. Collectibles Helper supports the Stones of
   Barenziah, Jiub's Opus, East Empire Pendants and Ancient Falmer Tomes collectibles. It also comes with
   functions in the MCM to simply put all undiscovered collectibles straight into your inventory
   instantaneously, except for East Empire Pendants.

Q. I ticked 'Show Map Markers' in the MCM but I don't see map any markers.
A. Skyrim can only show a limited number of map markers at a time, so you may have too many quests
   active in your journal. Turn a few of them off and ensure the respective Collectibles Helper
   miscellaneous objective is active.

Q. How do I remove a 'Collectibles Helper' miscellaneous objective from my journal?
A. Untick the respective Show Map Markers option in the MCM to remove the objective from your journal.

Q. I've disabled a collection quest in the MCM but I still see a quest objective for it.
A. If you've progressed the quest beyond the collecting phase this option will no longer remove the
   current objective from your journal since it would be kinda pointless at that stage.

Q. Can I modify this mod or use any assets from it?
A. You can do what ever you want with this mod, with attribution.

     6. Uninstallation

*****************************************************************

If you want to remove the mod permanently:

1. If you have SKSE installed and want to re-enable the Barenziah side quest, untick "Disable" in
   the MCM before closing the game.

2. Remove the Collectibles Helper.esp and Collectibles Helper.bsa files from your Skyrim data
   folder. By default this should be either of the following locations:

	C:\Program Files\Steam\steamapps\common\skyrim\data

			or for 64-bit Windows:

	C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\skyrim\data

3. Refer to SuperSajuuk's document on manually removing any left-over NSOB_* script files from
   your save file:

   https://www.dropbox.com/s/lj6d98yy8ohcoh3/Uninstalling%20Skyrim%20Mods.pdf?dl=0

   Note: You should not experience any problems with stability if you do not clean these script
   files from your save.
